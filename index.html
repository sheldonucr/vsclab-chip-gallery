The VLSI chips designed by VSCLAB @ UC Riverside

A gallery of physical designs taken from RTL to GDS‑II in the lab — processor cores, cryptographic engines, codecs, accelerators and memory blocks, hardened with both commercial and open-source tool flows across five technology nodes. Every layout below comes from an actual run: the images are the tools' own renderings, or the finished GDS‑II database drawn in KLayout.

About the data. Areas, cell counts, timing, wirelength, power and DRC figures are read directly from the reports each tool produced — Fusion Compiler report_design and report_qor summaries, and the OpenROAD flow's per-stage metrics files. Nothing is estimated.

About the images. OpenROAD designs show the flow's own layout renderings alongside a KLayout rendering of the final 6_final.gds tape-out database. Synopsys and OpenLane designs show layouts captured from the tool GUIs.

A note on the PDKs. ASAP7, FreePDK45/Nangate45 and the Synopsys SAED32 EDK are academic or predictive kits, not manufacturable processes. SKY130 and GF180MCU are real open-source foundry PDKs.
